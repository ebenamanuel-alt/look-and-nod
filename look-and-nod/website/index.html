How it works

Set it up once. Then just look and nod.

Your AirPods already sense how your head moves. Look and Nod turns that into which light you mean and what you want it to do.

  1. 1

    Recentre

    Put in your AirPods and press Recentre while facing your screen.

  2. 2

    Save each light

    Look at each light and press Save direction. The app remembers where every light is in the room.

  3. 3

    Hold, look, nod

    Hold Control and Option, look at a light and nod to switch it on or shake to switch it off. Add the up or down arrow to change its brightness.

What you need

Three things you may already own.

No extra hardware. If you have all three, you're ready.

The computer

A Mac

  • Running macOS 14 or newer
The sensors

AirPods that track your head

  • AirPods Pro
  • AirPods Max
  • AirPods 3rd generation or newer
The lights

Smart bulbs

  • Compatible with Tuya or Smart Life
  • For example TCP Smart
Why it's different

Where you look picks the light. A nod does the rest.

Head tracking and gaze control already exist. Here is how they compare.

ApproachWhat it does
Apple's AirPods head gesturesOnly answer calls and notifications.
Other AirPods motion appsMove the mouse or scroll.
Gaze controlled smart homesUsually need cameras or eye trackers.
Look and NodUses where you are facing to pick the light, and a nod to control it. No extra hardware.
