Look and Nod

Control the lights in this room with your head. Your webcam follows where you're pointing.

  • 👀Look at a lamp by turning your head towards it.
  • ↕️Nod to switch it on.
  • ↔️Shake your head to switch it off.
  • ⌨️↑ ↓ make the lamp you're looking at brighter or dimmer.

Your camera stays on your device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. I built the real version with AirPods head tracking and a smart bulb; this is the version anyone can try.
